Protecting Families Since 1999

TrustHold Insurance was founded by Robert and Carol Hartley with a simple idea: insurance should be something people understand, not fear. Starting with three agents and a two-room office in Austin, Texas, the agency grew steadily by putting clients first at every turn.

Today our team of 48 licensed agents (placeholder) serves clients across 12 states, handling everything from first-time renter policies to complex commercial coverage for mid-size businesses.

We are fiercely independent, which means we shop multiple carrier partners to find the best combination of coverage and price for you — not just the first policy that pays us a commission.
